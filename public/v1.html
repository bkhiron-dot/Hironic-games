Hardline MTB

1.2 km rainforest descent, four jumps, four timed splits.

How to ride
W / UpPedal (only helps below about 45 km/h) S / DownBrake A D / Left RightLean to steer. Hold longer to lean harder; tap for small corrections SpaceHold to compress, release to hop. Release right at a jump lip to pop REnd the run and see the high scores EnterDrop in

Lean with the arrows on the left. Pedal, brake and hop on the right.

Crashes. A small crash puts you back on the bike where you fell and the clock keeps running. A big crash (hitting a tree fast or casing a jump hard) sends you back to the top with the clock reset.

Corners. The tight ones have berms. Ride high on the berm wall and it holds you in at speed. Lean hard on flat dirt and the tyres let go and you drift wide. Drift up into a berm and it catches you and swings you back round the corner. Watch the grip bar under your speed.

Jumps. Land between the green flags for a smooth landing. Green to amber is the buffer, where your suspension works harder. Short of the amber flags you case it; past them you flat-land and bottom out. Normal jumps land smooth at about 46 to 68 km/h on the lip.

Big jumps have an orange sign before them. Hold Space up the kicker and release at the lip to pop; you'll reach the sweet spot at about 52 to 64 km/h. Without a pop you case it. The big jump after split 1 doesn't need a pop above about 52 km/h; pop it if you're slower.
