Hardline MTB

Hironic Games

Maydena Bike Park, Tasmania

Drop in at the trailhead and pick a line: ride through its gate. Flow Monster (blue): 1.3 km of rainforest berms, four jumps. Jolly Green Giant (green): 2 km, wide and flowing, with big rollers to pump and tabletops. Back in Black (black): narrow and twisty, bermed S-bends and six gaps you have to clear. All three finish in the same clearing.

Lean with the arrows on the left. Pedal, brake and hop on the right.

Trailhead. You start at rest in the clearing at the top. Pedal through the blue gate: the clock starts there.

Crashes. A small crash puts you back on the bike where you fell and the clock keeps running. A big crash (hitting a tree fast or casing a jump hard) ends the run.

Corners. The tight ones have berms. Ride high on the berm wall and it holds you in at speed. Lean hard on flat dirt and the tyres let go and you drift wide, losing a little speed. Keep steering into the corner and the berm catches you about halfway up: a berm hit. You grip again and rail round the berm. Stop steering, or steer the wrong way, and you drift out over the top. Watch the grip bar under your speed.

Jumps. Land between the green flags for a smooth landing. Green to amber is the buffer, where your suspension works harder. Short of the amber flags you case it; past them you flat-land and bottom out. The first jump and the one before split 3 are long: pop them at speed (smooth at about 48 to 68 and 54 to 78 km/h on the lip), or hit them flat out without a pop.

Rollers (Jolly Green Giant). Big rollers, for pumping. Hold Space (Hop on a phone) as you go over the top and down the back of each roller, and let go at the bottom as you start up the next one. Done well it's faster than pedalling: about 20-30 km/h faster over a set. Letting go at a crest doesn't launch you off these. Below about 18 km/h you won't get over one, so pedal or pump.

Tabletops (Jolly Green Giant). Roll over them, or pop the lip to clear the flat deck and land on the down-ramp. Come up short and you just land on the deck.

Gaps (Back in Black). Every jump must be cleared: gaps, step-downs (land lower than you take off) and step-ups (land higher). Fast, just ride them; slower, pop them; too slow and you case.

Switching trails. Ride out through another open gate at the trailhead to switch to that trail.

Big jumps have an orange sign before them. The one before split 2 clears without a pop above about 56 km/h, landing at the top of the landing; pop it and you land deep (smooth up to about 62). The last one needs a pop: come in fast (about 54 to 66 km/h at the lip), hold Space up the kicker and release at the lip. Aim your take-off at the landing: it's on a bend.
